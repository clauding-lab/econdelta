## Status interpretation

The six status families are **advancing**, **legitimate release lag**, **broken extraction**, **unavailable source**, **static/manual seed**, and **unsupported**. “Unverified source period” means the stored date/source was checked but the underlying document row was not independently certified in E0. Known source-period defects override a recent stored date. A no-row result is unsupported until a writer is verified.
